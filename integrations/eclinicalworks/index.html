What writes back to eClinicalWorks

After each call with an identified patient, VantageAI writes a telephone encounter with the call summary, a draft (unsigned) call-summary note, and, for new callers, a patient record with name, date of birth, and contact details. Appointments are not written directly into eClinicalWorks today.

  • Telephone encounter with call summary
  • Draft call-summary note (never signed)
  • New patient record (name, DOB, phone, email)

What does not write back

Mira handles calls and front-desk tasks. It never orders, prescribes, or bills, and its call notes are always unsigned drafts.

Frequently asked questions

Does VantageAI book on my real eClinicalWorks schedule?

Mira checks eClinicalWorks for booked visits before offering times, and calls from identified patients are logged to their chart as a telephone encounter. Direct appointment write-back into eClinicalWorks is on our roadmap.

What information writes back to eClinicalWorks?

A telephone encounter with the call summary, a draft (unsigned) call-summary note, and a new patient record when a caller isn't in eClinicalWorks yet. VantageAI does not write appointments directly into eClinicalWorks today. Mira never writes prescriptions, orders, or billing, and its call notes are always unsigned drafts.

What do I need to get started with eClinicalWorks?

You need eClinicalWorks FHIR API access: your practice authorizes VantageAI's apps in eClinicalWorks. Once access is in place, we configure the connection. Timing depends on how quickly access is authorized.

How long does setup take?

Once your practice has authorized VantageAI's apps in eClinicalWorks, we configure the connection. Timing depends on how quickly access is authorized.

What happens after hours?

Mira answers calls 24/7. Patients calling after hours still reach Mira, and their request is waiting for your team in eClinicalWorks.

Does VantageAI replace my front desk?

No. VantageAI covers the calls your team cannot get to—overflow during busy hours, after-hours calls, and missed callbacks. Your front desk stays in control.

What if we use a different EHR?

VantageAI currently integrates with eClinicalWorks and Open Dental. If you use a different system, contact us to discuss your options.
