Frequently asked questions

Does VantageAI book on my real eClinicalWorks schedule?

Yes. Mira reads your live eClinicalWorks schedule and books patients into actual open slots. No double-booking, no manual corrections.

What information writes back to eClinicalWorks?

Appointments and the patient and visit information needed to book them. VantageAI does not write clinical notes, charting, billing, or prescriptions back to eClinicalWorks.

What do I need to get started with eClinicalWorks?

You need eConnector or API access enabled with eClinicalWorks. Once that is in place, most practices are live within days.

How long does setup take?

Once your eClinicalWorks eConnector or API access is in place, most practices go live within days. The timeline depends on how quickly you can enable access with eClinicalWorks.

What happens after hours?

Mira answers calls 24/7. Patients calling after hours can still book, reschedule, or get answers—no voicemail, no missed opportunities.

Does VantageAI replace my front desk?

No. VantageAI covers the calls your team cannot get to—overflow during busy hours, after-hours calls, and missed callbacks. Your front desk stays in control.

What if we use a different EHR?

VantageAI currently integrates with eClinicalWorks and Open Dental. If you use a different system, contact us to discuss your options.
